A beginner course in Python and pygame-ce

Wireframe 3D

Short lessons that grow one small program, from a single line on the screen to a 3D game you can fly round, and then make its engine stronger. You only need basic Python. Every idea comes with pictures, worked numbers and fully commented code.

The lessons

  1. Lesson 1From one line to a 3D cube

    You will draw a line, then a shape made of points and edges, then a real 3D cube on a flat screen.

    Code: step1_line.py step2_polygon.py step3_cube.py

  2. Lesson 2Making the cube spin

    Lesson 1 ended with a cube that only faces you.

    Code: step1_sin_cos.py step2_turn_cube.py step3_spin_cube.py

  3. Lesson 2BTip, roll and tumble

    Lesson 2 turned the cube around one skewer, the upright y axis.

    Code: step1_tip_cube.py step2_roll_cube.py step3_tumble_cube.py

  4. Lesson 3Solid faces

    So far the cube has been made of wire: 12 lines with nothing between them.

    Code: step1_fill_faces.py step2_painter.py step3_hide_back_faces.py step4_lit_cube.py

  5. Lesson 4Moving the camera

    Until now the cube did all the moving and you sat still.

    Code: step1_walk.py step2_turn.py step3_look_around.py

  6. Lesson 5A small world

    One cube in empty space makes it hard to feel that you are moving.

    Code: step1_two_shapes.py step2_size_and_spin.py step3_small_world.py

  7. Lesson 6Matrices

    Since Lesson 2 every turn has been its own little formula: tip, turn, roll, then undo your turn and undo your tip.

    Code: step1_matrix_by_hand.py step2_spin_matrix.py step3_camera_matrix.py

  8. Lesson 7A mini game: Cube Hunt

    Six lessons built a 3D world you can fly round.

    Code: step1_cube_field.py step2_collect.py step3_cube_hunt.py

  9. Lesson 8Clipping: cutting faces at the near wall

    Fly right up beside something big in Cube Hunt and a whole wall of it can vanish.

    Code: step1_rocks.py step2_clip_lines.py step3_clipped_hunt.py

  10. Lesson 10The depth buffer: closest wins, block by block

    Since Lesson 3 the program has drawn solid shapes with the painter's order: sort whole faces far to near, then paint them in turn.

    Code: step1_fill_by_hand.py step2_depth_buffer.py step3_cube_hunt_depth.py

  11. Lesson 11Movement and collisions

    Cube Hunt works, but moving round it feels like sliding a cursor: full speed the instant you press W, stopped dead the instant you let go, and nothing in the world is solid.

    Code: step1_velocity.py step2_gravity.py step3_crates.py
