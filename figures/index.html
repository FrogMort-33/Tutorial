Fig 3.4.1b is Lesson 3, Part 4 (hide the back faces), the 1st figure in that part, panel b: the x-ray view of all 6 faces.

  • Lesson is 1, 2, 2B, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 or 8. Lesson 2B keeps its own name, so 2B.1.3 can never be mixed up with 2.1.3.
  • Part is the "Part N" heading on the lesson page. 0 is everything before Part 1 (course map, lesson plan, recap). W is the wrap up at the end.
  • Figure counts 1, 2, 3 inside that part, in page order. Pictures and tables share the count, so Table 1.4.1 and Fig 1.4.2 sit side by side and no number is used twice.
  • Panel is a letter for one picture inside a PNG that holds several (a filmstrip, a left and right pair, a row of cards). Letters run in reading order: left to right, then top to bottom. Each caption names its panels, for example 3.4.1a (left).
  • A picture used twice on one page gets two numbers; the second caption says "Same picture as Fig …".
