  • Lesson is 1, 2, 2B, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 or 11. Lesson 2B keeps its own name, so 2B.1.3 can never be mixed up with 2.1.3.
  • Part is the "Part N" heading on the lesson page. 0 is everything before Part 1 (course map, lesson plan, recap). W is the wrap up at the end.
  • Figure counts 1, 2, 3 inside that part, in page order. Pictures and tables share the count, so Table 1.4.1 and Fig 1.4.2 sit side by side and no number is used twice.
